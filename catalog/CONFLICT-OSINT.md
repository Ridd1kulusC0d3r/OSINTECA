# Conflict Verification OSINT

OSINTECA treats conflict-related OSINT as a **verification, documentation and public-interest analysis** discipline.

## Scope

Use this section for:

- source verification and provenance;
- geolocation of publicly documented events;
- chronology and timeline reconstruction;
- media authenticity checks;
- satellite-based change detection for public-interest reporting;
- humanitarian and human-rights documentation;
- misinformation and disinformation analysis;
- preservation of public evidence.

## Red lines

OSINTECA does not provide operational guidance for locating, obtaining, using or targeting restricted items, people or facilities. Public-source observations must not be converted into tactical targeting advice.

## Evidence workflow

1. Define the intelligence requirement and public-interest purpose.
2. Preserve the original source and timestamp.
3. Separate observation, claim, corroboration and inference.
4. Cross-check with independent sources.
5. Record geolocation/time confidence and uncertainty.
6. Preserve the chain of provenance.
7. Publish only what is necessary and proportionate.
